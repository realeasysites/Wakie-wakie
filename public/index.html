Our story · you made it to the moon

“To the moon.”

Wakie Wakie's began with a simple dream shared by three women with a deep love for cooking and a passion for clean, fresh food — organic whenever possible.

Thai food is our culture, and we know it can be light, fresh and good for you. So we built a breakfast spot around it: a cozy room on Main Street filled with hand-painted art, where every dish is approachable and every smoothie gives your morning a lift.

“To the moon” is our vision — to share our healthy journey and our culture with this community, one plate at a time.

— the Wakie Wakie's crew ✦
